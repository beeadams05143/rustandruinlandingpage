Meet the Duo

Rust and Ruin Duo delivers big energy in a small package — think vintage vinyl grooves, rich harmonies, and a retro vibe that fills the room. As a husband-and-wife team, they bring chemistry and connection that keeps audiences smiling, dancing, and singing along. Perfect for intimate settings that still want a full, lively sound.
