RUST AND RUIN — BAND BIO

Rust and Ruin is a retro ’70s band from Chester, Vermont, bringing the unmistakable sound and spirit of the decade to audiences throughout New England and Upstate New York. Inspired by artists including the Eagles, Linda Ronstadt, Crosby, Stills & Nash, Creedence Clearwater Revival, the Rolling Stones, and the Grateful Dead, the group blends rock, country, and folk into a dynamic live show filled with powerful vocals, memorable guitar work, and tightly interwoven harmonies.

Husband-and-wife team Josh and Beth Adams began performing together in 2012 and formed Rust and Ruin in 2018. Today, they are joined by bassist and vocalist Gary Aldrich, drummer and backing vocalist Todd Westfall, and backline technician Jennifer Durkee. Together, they have developed a reputation as one of the most sought-after live acts in New England and Upstate New York.

Rust and Ruin is available as a full band, an acoustic duo, and through specially designed themed shows that capture the excitement of a tribute experience while celebrating the music of an entire era rather than focusing on a single artist. Their diverse repertoire, audience interaction, and authentic love of live music make every performance unique. Whether appearing on a festival stage, at a fair, in a concert setting, or at an intimate venue, the band approaches every performance with three goals: engage the audience, get people on their feet, and leave them wanting more.

Rust and Ruin has appeared at prominent events and venues including the Altamont Balloon Festival, the 100th Anniversary of Laconia Motorcycle Week, the Cornish Fair, the Cheshire Fair, the Enfield Fair, the Vermont State Fair, Addison County Fair & Field Days, and the Tunbridge World’s Fair, among many others.

In addition to performing an extensive catalog of retro favorites, Rust and Ruin writes and records original music. The band’s original material has been featured regionally on WCAX Channel 3, broadcast on WOOL Black Sheep Radio and Wigwam Radio, and released through all major streaming platforms. Their growing catalog includes earlier releases such as “Don’t Lose the Light,” “Friday Eve,” “Name on the Breeze,” “It’s Not a Question,” “Stuck in the Past,” and “Never Say Goodbye,” along with more recent projects including “Winds of Mars,” “Raise the Stakes,” “Reckless Abandon,” and their Christmas album.

MEET RUST AND RUIN

JOSH ADAMS — VOCALS AND ELECTRIC GUITAR

Josh Adams first picked up a guitar at age fifteen, inspired by players such as Joe Walsh, Billy Gibbons, and John Fogerty. Music runs in his family: his grandfather played organ, his uncle played banjo, and his mother and aunts sang.

After studying music theory in high school and serving in the Navy, Josh performed with several cover bands, including Chaser, Aftershock, and HighWire. Following several years as a solo acoustic performer, he formed the outlaw country and Southern rock band DV8 with Beth in 2011. In 2018, Josh and Beth assembled a new lineup and launched Rust and Ruin.

Decades of experience in the local music scene have sharpened Josh’s ability to connect with an audience. His powerful voice, commanding guitar work, and unmistakable retro style help define the Rust and Ruin sound.

BETH ADAMS — VOCALS AND ACOUSTIC GUITAR

Beth Adams—the older of identical twins by five minutes—grew up surrounded by music. Although her childhood included time in Texas, California, Idaho, and Vermont, some of her strongest memories are rooted in family music: listening to her Uncle Joe’s band rehearse and perform at the local Big Wheel Pizza and falling asleep while her grandmother, uncle, and their friends played guitar and sang late into the night.

Singing has always been a source of comfort and expression for Beth. Her musical influences draw heavily from Laurel Canyon and Haight-Ashbury, including the vocal harmonies of the Eagles and the Beatles; the songwriting of Crosby, Stills & Nash, Bob Dylan, and Neil Young; the improvisation of the Grateful Dead; the grit of the Rolling Stones; and the swampy sound of Creedence Clearwater Revival. Vocally, she is inspired by Linda Ronstadt, Dolly Parton, Grace Slick, and Stevie Nicks.

In addition to singing and playing acoustic guitar, Beth manages the band’s branding, marketing, and business operations. She also writes and records original music with Rust and Ruin, helping shape both the band’s creative direction and its connection with audiences.

GARY ALDRICH — BASS AND VOCALS

Any biography of Gary Aldrich begins with his wife, Jennifer Durkee. Her support has made his musical career possible, and the two are an essential part of the Rust and Ruin family.

Based in Walpole, New Hampshire, Gary has sung and played bass with local groups including Resonance, which appeared at the Keene Pumpkin Festival, and the acoustic trio Back Porch Bandits. He has a deep appreciation for music in all its forms, with influences that include punk and hardcore bands such as the Ramones, Descendents, and Suicidal Tendencies.

Gary brings a distinctive musical perspective, strong vocals, and a solid foundation to Rust and Ruin’s blend of rock, country, and folk.

JENNIFER DURKEE — BACKLINE TECHNICIAN

Jennifer Durkee is the behind-the-scenes glue that helps hold Rust and Ruin together. As the band’s backline technician, she keeps equipment, stage needs, and countless practical details under control before, during, and after each performance.

Jennifer has an extraordinary ability to remain calm, solve problems, and handle the unexpected emergencies that are an inevitable part of live music. Her support, reliability, and quick thinking allow the musicians to concentrate on performing. Simply put, Rust and Ruin would not be able to rock without her.

TODD WESTFALL — DRUMS AND BACKING VOCALS

A lifelong resident of the Bellows Falls, Vermont, area, Todd Westfall began his musical journey in 2002 as a founding member of Spectris. During the next fifteen years, he developed his skills as both a drummer and vocalist while performing classic, progressive, hard, modern, and alternative rock.

After Spectris disbanded, Todd moved into country rock with Common Ground, performing with the Sharon, Vermont-area group from 2018 until 2020. He joined the Rust and Ruin family in late 2021, initially recording drum tracks for several releases before beginning live performances with the band in early 2022.

A seasoned drummer and vocalist, Todd contributes complex rhythms, musical versatility, and tight vocal harmonies to Rust and Ruin. He also performs with the country and oldies project Marty & Todd and is a highly sought-after fill-in drummer within the local music community.

Todd is also the band’s resident joker, rarely missing an opportunity to make the rest of the group laugh. In his own words, he enjoys long walks on the beach, honey buns, working with tools, listening to Tool, and—most of all—being a tool. His humor and easygoing personality are as much a part of the Rust and Ruin family as his drumming.

Together, Josh, Beth, Gary, Jennifer, and Todd bring experience, personality, musicianship, and genuine camaraderie to every Rust and Ruin performance. Their chemistry—onstage and behind the scenes—is at the heart of a show designed to transport audiences back to the glory days of the 1970s while keeping the experience immediate, lively, and unmistakably their own.
