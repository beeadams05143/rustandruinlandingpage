Rust and Ruin brings the sound and spirit of the 1970s to life with powerful vocals, rich harmonies, and an energetic mix of classic rock, country rock, and unforgettable favorites. Based in Southern Vermont, Rust and Ruin performs as a full band or acoustic duo and also offers themed shows that deliver the feel of a tribute experience while celebrating the music of an entire era rather than a single artist. Every show is built around authentic live music, audience connection, and a great night out.
